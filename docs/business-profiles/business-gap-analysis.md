# Twenty-business gap analysis

This is a repository-based capability assessment, not a claim that every listed engine is a complete vertical product. Existing means an implementation is present; partial and missing identify the work needed before making industry-specific product promises. Profile names do not make unavailable engines functional.

P0 preserves production foundations; P1 covers broadly shared next capabilities; P2 adds deeper optional engines; P3 covers specialised workflows.

## Next shared capabilities by overlap

1. **Sales, quotes and proposals (P1).** Most service, agency, consultancy, construction, logistics, property and professional businesses need estimates, line items, acceptance and conversion into work. Add this above the existing customer and payment engines.
2. **Work/jobs and a reusable lifecycle (P1).** Shared by trades, cleaning, logistics, maintenance, garages, charity cases and professional engagements. Build assignment, status transitions, due dates, tasks, forms, attachments and an activity timeline before dedicated dispatch or industry apps.
3. **Customer relationships and universal CRM fields (P1).** Add organisation/person relationships, guardians, owners, status, source, tags and an auditable activity timeline. Keep existing clients and sensitive salon data intact.
4. **Documents and approvals (P1).** Reuse forms and existing file capabilities through governed attachments, document versions, customer acceptance, tenant scope and capability-aware access.
5. **Invoices, recurrence and contracts (P1/P2).** Extend existing payments/finance with invoice lifecycle, recurring commitments and clear money semantics. Do not replace payment history or invent accounting balances.
6. **Assets and inventory movements (P1/P2).** Vehicles, properties, equipment and parts share ownership, identifiers, assignments and history. Deliver a bounded asset engine before a general custom-object platform.
7. **Projects, classes and dispatch as optional engines (P2).** Projects need milestones and dependencies; classes need capacity, cohorts and attendance; dispatch needs routes, vehicles and driver workflows. They can share work, people, assets, documents and payments.
8. **AI recommendations (later).** Build reliable statuses, due dates, ownership and activity first. Never generate operational metrics or advice from absent engines.

## Deferred in this phase

Full sales CRM/pipelines, custom objects, fleet, route optimisation, driver apps, social publishing, universal project management, a new finance platform, a new communications platform and AI next-best-action. No separate industry applications are introduced.
