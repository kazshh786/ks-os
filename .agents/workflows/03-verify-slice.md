# Verify Migrated Slice

Verify:
- unauthenticated access is rejected where required;
- users cannot access another tenant's data;
- agency-only actions cannot be performed by staff or owners;
- validation rejects malformed requests;
- loading, empty, error, and success states render;
- refresh and deep links work;
- no browser console errors occur;
- tests, type-check, and production builds pass;
- no real secrets appear in source control.

Provide evidence for each check and label anything not tested.
